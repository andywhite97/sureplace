Finding a placeContact an owner or agent safelyUse the contact options on the listing and verify details before committing.
  1. Open the property or stay and use the contact options shown on that listing.
  2. Ask questions, arrange a viewing where appropriate, and keep written communication for reference.
  3. Never rely on a badge alone or send money before independently confirming the listing, provider and terms. SurePlace verification is not a guarantee of a transaction.

Stays & bookingsRequest a booking for a stayChoose an available room and dates, then send the host a booking request.
  1. On a stay page, select a room and dates with availability.
  2. Review the dates, nightly prices and total shown before continuing.
  3. Sign in and submit the guest details to send your request. A request is not a confirmed reservation; check its status in your account and follow up with the host if needed.
  4. SurePlace does not take payment at this stage. Confirm payment arrangements and terms directly with the provider.

  3. Use the conversation associated with the booking, when available, to communicate with the host.

Listing with SurePlaceCreate a property listingStart a property draft, add its details and photos, then submit it for review.
  1. Sign in and open Manage Properties, then choose to create a property.
  2. Complete the required information including property type, sale or rental details, location and price.
  3. Add photos. The property form requires listing details and at least one uploaded photo before submission.
  4. Review your information, confirm it is accurate and submit the listing for review.

Listing with SurePlaceCreate a stay listingSet up accommodation details and rooms, then submit the stay for review.
  1. Sign in and open Manage Stays to start a stay listing.
  2. Add the accommodation details, contact information and at least one room with its availability and pricing information.
  3. Review the listing and confirm the details before submitting it for review.
  4. You can return to Manage Stays to continue working on your listings.

Listing with SurePlaceUnderstand verification badgesBadges indicate evidence reviewed by SurePlace; they are useful signals, not guarantees.
  1. Read the verification information to understand the kinds of checks represented by badges.
  2. For your own verification, open the verification area in your account and follow the requirements shown for your request.
  3. If a request needs changes, read the staff note, update the information or documents, and submit again.
  4. Always do your own checks before a transaction; a badge does not guarantee a person, property, stay or outcome.
